Our story

Made by a woman who couldn't find activewear that fit her.

GENiO was founded in Nairobi in 2019 by Corazon Kwamboka, who still leads it as founder and CEO. A lover of sport and the gym, and a curvy size 16/18, she found that international brands didn't cater to her. So she started making her own.

That idea shapes every piece today: activewear for women of all sizes who want function and fashion in the same outfit. High-waisted, super stretchy, and sized from S to XXXL.
